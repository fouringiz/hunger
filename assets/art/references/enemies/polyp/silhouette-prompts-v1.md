## Attempt 1

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one landscape concept sheet for Polyp, an alien swarm enemy for a 2.5D tower defense game.
Create THREE DISTINCT ORIGINAL BLACK SILHOUETTE alternatives in a clean three-column layout, labelled only A, B, C. Each column contains one large silhouette, and beneath it an identical tiny silhouette for thumbnail comparison. Pure solid black creatures on clean warm-white background, no shading, no texture, no grey interiors, no anatomy linework, no cast shadows. White negative spaces and gaps between fused lobes are allowed and important.
Reference image roles: image 1 shows stretched connecting tissue; image 2 (many-armed flesh mass) shows recognisable constituent bodies trapped in a whole; image 3 (interlocked body sculpture) shows different bodies holding each other together; image 4 shows wildly conflicting directions of pulls. These are MOOD and FUSION-STRUCTURE references ONLY. Do NOT copy their human anatomy.
Subject in each column: six distinct folded flat disc organisms grafted around a seventh load-bearing soft central body. Each folded disc is a palpable LOBE, held in place by its own thick flexible tapering hydrostatic tentacles; adjacent tentacles tangle, fuse and stretch between neighbours. The shape must read immediately as several wrongly joined organisms pulling in different directions, NOT a single animal, not a flower, not an octopus, not a spider. Six constituent lobes must substantially shape the OUTER CONTOUR, not disappear inside one round blob. A few broad stretched bridges and irregular negative spaces between lobes sell forced fusion. Compact, grounded, heavy, no upright pose. Some tentacles can point outward in conflicting directions but keep them stout enough to survive 50px thumbnail scale.
A: compact asymmetric knot, six bulky folded disc lobes compressed into a heavy approximately round but strongly scalloped crawling heap, short hooked soft loops binding them.
B: lowest widest colony, six lobes spread unequally around central carrier, two lobes dragging behind, tangled low tentacles weaving between masses; broad footprint, irregular gaps, no regular six-point star.
C: skewed tumbling mass, six unevenly stacked folded lobes leaning off-centre, different lobes pulling different ways, lower lobe compressed under weight; organic lurching outline, not a humanoid.
All three seen at the SAME elevated three-quarter camera, roughly 35 degrees downward, showing top and side, matching 2.5D tower platforms. Clearly distinct silhouettes with equal visual area. No ground plane.
Mandatory exclusions: NO humans, faces, heads, eyes, teeth, hands, fingers, torsos, upright humanoid proportions; NO articulated insect/spider/crab legs, bones or armour shells; NO metal, cables, blood, gore, graphic injury. Entirely fictional nonhuman soft marine-like anatomy. Body horror conveyed ONLY by forced asymmetrical fusion and conflicting body directions.
This is silhouette exploration, not finished colored art or a gameplay screenshot. Keep plenty of white margin and no labels other than A B C.
