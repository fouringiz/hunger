Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one square seamless weathered ancient stone material texture for a monumental sci-fi portal in an overhead tower-defense game. Only flat surface material edge to edge. Earthy neutral gray / warm brown stone, eroded dark recesses, fine mineral granules, small chips, old worn geometric incisions and traces of olive moss. It must read as ancient stone/unknown mineral technology, not rusty modern metal, flesh, paving blocks or medieval castle wall masonry. No objects, lighting halo, big central symbol, portal, environment, perspective, shadows, border or text. Even overhead illumination, subdued highlights. Fine detail readable on small projected architectural surfaces. Opposite edges seamless. This material will be applied to actual upright 3D stone blocks with separate top/side face lighting.
