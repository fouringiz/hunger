# Forager / living mire motion study v2

The first thin segmented/stick-leg motion sample was rejected by the player. Its opt-in game renderer, seed and movie have been removed from this PR; they remain recoverable in commit `6129568`. This revision is a separate art/motion study for one Forager, before game integration.

## Review

Run `node tools/preview-forager.cjs`, then open `reports/generated/forager-study.html` and reload the page if it was already open. Playback can be paused or slowed down.

- Eight distinct key poses, in a 2-column / 4-row atlas, 506×378 pixels per frame; one shared crop and scale, no per-frame resizing.
- Playback: eight poses per second, one-second cycle. The MP4 repeats the cycle five times at 24 encoded frames per second using repeated poses, not optical-flow morphing.
- One grounded creature: hooked feet reach/plant/pull, belly contracts, slime bridges move and contact mud overlaps the lower silhouette.
- Brown peat/umber ground has less saturation and contrast than the maroon flesh and bone shell, per player feedback. Organic folds and shared contact slime remain visible.
- This includes painted ground and lighting. It is not yet a transparent game sprite, a navigation change or a completed terrain renderer. Registration and anatomy still need a human motion review before extraction/integration.

## Production

Built-in imagegen generated all poses together from the approved Forager/living-mire reference. One subsequent edit pulled every camera back consistently to restore clipped tips. A final material edit changed the terrain to swamp-brown while preserving unit color and all poses. Each atlas cell was cropped by three pixels on every edge to remove the sheet gutters, then packed without changing its anchor. The first interpolation experiment was discarded because it ghosted the limbs; delivered files use the drawn poses only.
